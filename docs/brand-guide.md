# BizTrust Brand Identity Guide v1.0

**Brand:** BizTrust  
**Descriptor in current artwork:** Broker Insurance  
**Document status:** Draft brand standard derived from supplied SVG/PNG master artwork  
**Primary typeface in supplied SVG:** Inter

---

## 1. Brand Foundation

### Brand idea

**Trust made operational.**

BizTrust should present insurance as clear, controlled, accessible, and digitally enabled. The identity should communicate protection without looking conservative, and technology without looking cold.

### Recommended positioning

BizTrust is an insurance brokerage platform and service brand designed to connect customers, brokers, insurers, and partner organizations through a trusted digital operating layer.

### Brand attributes

- **Trusted** — credible, controlled, transparent.
- **Modern** — digital-first and future-ready.
- **Clear** — easy to understand, never unnecessarily complex.
- **Connected** — bridges customers, brokers, insurers, and partners.
- **Capable** — professional enough for enterprise, approachable enough for customers.

### Visual interpretation of the mark

The symbol can be consistently interpreted as a combination of:

- a **shield / protection frame**;
- an abstract **B/T monogram**;
- a **bridge or pathway** representing brokerage and connection;
- a continuous **ribbon / flow** representing service continuity and digital movement.

These interpretations are recommended brand semantics; the SVG artwork itself does not encode an official narrative.

## 2. Logo System

### Supplied master assets

1. **Symbol-only mark** — `logo.svg` / `logo.png`
2. **Horizontal lockup** — `logoXhText.svg` / `logoXhText.png`
3. **Vertical lockup** — `logoXvText.svg` / `logoXvText.png`

### Usage hierarchy

- **Primary corporate/application logo:** horizontal lockup.
- **Secondary / centered composition:** vertical lockup.
- **Compact use:** symbol-only mark.
- **Favicon, app icon, avatar, watermark:** symbol-only mark.

### File preference

Use **SVG first** for websites, apps, presentations, print layouts, and scalable digital surfaces. Use PNG only when a raster format is required.

---

## 3. Logo Construction and Clear Space

Define **X = 1/6 of the symbol width**.

Maintain at least **1X clear space** on every side of the symbol or complete lockup. No text, icon, border, photograph edge, or UI control should enter this area.

For premium or executive applications, use **1.5X clear space** where space permits.

### Minimum size — recommended operational standard

| Variant           | Digital minimum | Print minimum |
| ----------------- | --------------: | ------------: |
| Symbol only       |      32 px high |     8 mm high |
| Horizontal lockup |     180 px wide |    45 mm wide |
| Vertical lockup   |     150 px wide |    32 mm wide |

If the descriptor “Broker Insurance” is no longer clearly readable, switch to the symbol-only mark rather than shrinking the lockup further.

### Signature gradient

**`#A5DEF3 → #69C6B5 → #3DB389`**

Use this three-stop gradient for:

- the symbol;
- the “Trust” wordmark treatment where supplied;
- selected key-line brand moments;
- high-value hero graphics and product accents.

Do not substitute random aqua/green gradients. The exact three stops are part of the identity.

### Gradient direction

Preserve the directional behavior of the master artwork for the logo itself. For supporting graphics, use a light-to-deep transition that feels continuous rather than radial, metallic, or neon.

## 5. Accessibility and Digital Color Use

The lighter identity colors are excellent brand accents but are not dark enough to function as body-text colors on white.

Approximate contrast ratios against white:

- `#A5DEF3`: **1.46:1**
- `#69C6B5`: **2.03:1**
- `#3DB389`: **2.62:1**
- `#2F302F`: **13.25:1**
- `#40403F`: **10.38:1**

Therefore:

- use **Carbon / Graphite** for body text on white;
- do not place white body text directly over the core mint/green colors and assume accessibility;
- use the signature gradient mainly for identity, illustration, border, icon, and large decorative treatment.

### Recommended accessible digital extension

For primary CTA backgrounds requiring white text, introduce a darker functional teal such as **`#0B7A68`**. This is a recommended digital extension, not a color extracted from the supplied logo artwork.

## 6. Typography

The supplied SVG specifies **Inter**.

### Primary family

**Inter**

### Logo typography in source artwork

- **Biz** — Inter Bold / 700
- **Broker Insurance** — Inter Light / 300
- **Trust** — rendered as vector paths in the supplied horizontal SVG and visually aligned with the bold wordmark treatment.

### Recommended type scale

- **Display / hero:** Inter Bold 700
- **H1:** Inter Bold 700
- **H2:** Inter SemiBold 600
- **H3:** Inter SemiBold 600
- **Body:** Inter Regular 400
- **Labels / UI controls:** Inter Medium 500
- **Supporting captions:** Inter Regular 400
- **Premium descriptor / editorial accent:** Inter Light 300

### Typographic behavior

Use generous spacing, short line lengths, and strong hierarchy. BizTrust should feel precise and calm rather than dense or bureaucratic.

## 7. Background Rules

### Preferred

- white;
- very light neutral backgrounds;
- clean, low-noise photography with sufficient negative space;
- dark neutral surfaces when the logo has been specifically prepared for that background.

### Avoid

- placing the full-color logo on a busy photograph;
- low-contrast aqua/green backgrounds behind the gradient mark;
- patterns that interfere with the internal negative space of the symbol;
- unapproved shadows, glows, outlines, or boxes around the logo.

A dedicated one-color/reversed logo master should be created before standardized use on very dark or monochrome applications.

---

## 8. Logo Misuse

Never:

- stretch or compress the logo;
- rotate the mark;
- rebuild the wordmark with another font;
- recolor individual ribbon sections arbitrarily;
- change the gradient stops;
- apply drop shadows, bevels, 3D effects, or glow;
- crop the shield shape;
- place text inside the symbol;
- rearrange the horizontal lockup without an approved master;
- alter the spacing between Biz, Trust, and the descriptor;
- change “BizTrust” to “Biz Trust” in brand-facing copy.

---

## 9. Naming and Brand Architecture

### Canonical name

**BizTrust**

Use capital **B** and **T** in brand-facing copy.

### Current descriptor

**Broker Insurance**

The supplied logo artwork uses “Broker Insurance.” Treat this as the locked visual descriptor unless the brand language is intentionally revised across all assets.

### Endorsed form — when corporate attribution is needed

**BizTrust by BizEra**

The endorsement should be secondary to BizTrust and should not compete with the BizTrust wordmark.

## 10. Voice and Tone

### Core voice

**Clear. Assured. Human. Precise.**

BizTrust should sound like a trusted adviser supported by a modern platform—not like a legal document and not like an aggressive sales campaign.

### Communication principles

- Explain before persuading.
- Make coverage and conditions understandable.
- Use evidence and specifics rather than hype.
- Separate confirmed coverage from estimates or assumptions.
- Communicate security and control without fear-based messaging.
- Make next actions explicit.

### Good examples

- “Compare coverage. Understand the terms. Choose with confidence.”
- “One trusted platform for brokerage, policy operations, and partner coordination.”
- “Clear coverage. Controlled access. Reliable service.”

### Avoid

- “The cheapest insurance guaranteed.”
- “100% safe.”
- “Zero risk.”
- “Best insurance in the country” unless independently substantiated.

## 11. Visual Language Beyond the Logo

### Shapes

Use soft geometric structures inspired by the logo:

- shield contours;
- flowing ribbons;
- rounded pathways;
- protected enclosures;
- connected nodes and bridges.

### Graphic treatment

Favor:

- controlled whitespace;
- one dominant visual idea per composition;
- light aqua-to-green gradient accents;
- charcoal typography;
- rounded but disciplined geometry.

Avoid generic “fintech neon,” excessive glassmorphism, or overly playful insurance imagery.

- rounded line icons;

- simple geometry;

- policy;
- shield/protection;
- customer;
- broker;

- verification;

---

## 13. Photography and Illustration

### Photography

Prefer authentic, modern, human imagery showing:

- customers receiving support;
- professional brokers/advisers;
- business and mobility contexts;
- travel, vehicle, health, property, and commercial protection scenarios where relevant.

Avoid cliché handshake photos, staged fear imagery, and imagery that implies a coverage guarantee not supported by the product.

### Illustration

Use flat or softly dimensional vector illustration with the BizTrust palette. Build flows and systems from ribbon/pathway motifs rather than cartoon mascots.

```css
:root {
  --bt-sky: #a5def3;
  --bt-mint: #69c6b5;
  --bt-green: #3db389;
  --bt-carbon: #2f302f;
  --bt-graphite: #40403f;
  --bt-ink: #1e1e1e;
  --bt-white: #ffffff;

  /* Recommended functional extension for accessible CTA surfaces */
  --bt-teal-accessible: #0b7a68;

  --bt-gradient: linear-gradient(90deg, #a5def3 0%, #69c6b5 50%, #3db389 100%);
}
```

### UI hierarchy

- **Primary text:** Carbon
- **Secondary text:** Graphite
- **Primary CTA:** accessible dark teal, with white text
- **Brand highlight:** signature gradient
- **Cards/surfaces:** white or very light neutral
- **Borders:** neutral gray; use brand colors sparingly
- **Success:** keep functionally distinct from brand green where status ambiguity is possible
- **Warning / error:** use dedicated semantic colors, not modified BizTrust gradients

## 15. Application Rules

### Website header

Use the **horizontal lockup** on desktop. Use the symbol-only mark for compact mobile navigation only when “BizTrust” remains visible elsewhere in the interface or brand recognition is established.

### App icon

Use the **symbol-only mark**, centered with generous padding. Do not insert the wordmark into the app icon.

### Social avatar

Use the symbol-only mark on a clean light or approved dark background.

### Presentations

Use the horizontal lockup on title and closing slides. Use the symbol-only mark as a small footer/signature mark on internal slides.

### Formal documents

Use the horizontal lockup in the header or cover. Maintain clear space and avoid placing it next to dense regulatory text.

### Partner / co-branding

Keep BizTrust and partner logos optically equal unless contractual hierarchy requires otherwise. Separate logos with generous whitespace or a neutral divider. Never merge partner colors into the BizTrust mark.

---

## 16. Brand Governance

Before publication, verify:

1. Correct approved logo asset is used.
2. Logo proportions are unchanged.
3. Required clear space is maintained.
4. Correct gradient stops are used.
5. Inter is used for brand typography.
6. Body text meets accessibility requirements.
7. “BizTrust” naming is consistent.
8. Descriptor is unchanged unless formally approved.
9. Claims are evidence-based and do not imply unsupported guarantees.
10. Partner and co-brand arrangements maintain clear brand ownership.
