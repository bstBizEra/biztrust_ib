# Laos insurance product website research

Collected 26 September 2026. This is a source-linked research dataset for the BizTrust catalog, not a list of approved brokerage offers. The product CSV is [`../data/laos_insurance_products_research.csv`](../data/laos_insurance_products_research.csv); the insurer research roster is [`../data/laos_insurer_source_roster.csv`](../data/laos_insurer_source_roster.csv).

## Scope and method

- Searched for insurers operating in Laos and reviewed the insurers' own public product pages or Laos-specific corporate material. A 2026 [Insurance Directory of Asia listing](https://apps.asiainsurancereview.com/IDA/Asp/CompanyList.aspx?country=Laos&search=company) was used to discover company names; it is not treated as a live regulator license register. [Axco's Laos market page](https://www.axcoinfo.com/countries/asia-pacific/laos/) reports 23 licensed companies in 2024, so this dataset is not an exhaustive list of licensed insurers.
- Recorded the exact public name shown where possible. `listing_level` distinguishes a named product, a plan variant, a rider, and a broad insurance line. A broad line must not be presented to customers as a comparable plan.
- `public_price_lak` is populated only when a price is displayed by the insurer. It may be a promotion, a starting price, or depend on the customer and coverage. It is not a quote. Blank means no price was captured, not that the product is free.
- Every row is `Research only`. A product lead still needs to verify the policy wording, current availability, eligibility, limits, exclusions, deductibles, premium basis, distribution rights, and insurer identity before publication or quoting.
- No product rows were added from a parent company's Thailand or Cambodia catalog unless the source explicitly identified a Laos offer. This matters for [Forte Laos](https://www.forteinsurance.com/media/4425/forte-group-corporate-profile-2024-2.pdf): its Laos-specific corporate profile supports general insurance lines, while the group's named online plans mostly target Cambodia.

## Coverage snapshot

The CSV has 100 entries from 11 insurers' public sites or Laos-specific corporate material. The strongest plan-level public data was found at [Lao-Viet Insurance](https://www.lvi.la/en), [APA Insurance](https://apa.com.la/), [AGL](https://www.agl.com.la/), [Prudential Laos](https://www.prudential.la/corp/prudential-la/en/life-insurance/), [MSIG Sokxay](https://msig-sokxay.com/en/insurance-products/), and [Lanexang Assurance](https://www.lap.com.la/). [Bangkok Insurance Lao](https://www.bkilao.com/products.html), [TKI](https://tki-insurance.com/?lang=en), [Laovivat](https://laovivat.com/), and [Indochina Insurance](https://icilaos.com/) mainly exposed categories in this pass.

## Companies needing follow-up

The [2026 directory](https://apps.asiainsurancereview.com/IDA/Asp/CompanyList.aspx?country=Laos&search=company) also lists Champa Insurance, Vientiane Insurance, Zhongji Insurance, and separate TKI general/life companies; no dependable product catalog was captured for these in this pass. [Champa's cited website](https://www.champa.com.la/) was inaccessible through the research browser. The public [ST-Muang Thai general site](https://www.stmtins.com/) has a June 2026 notice about transferring non-life policies, so its product availability needs direct confirmation. Dhipaya Laos' [public site](https://dhipaya.la/) showed internal system links rather than a customer product catalog. These are research gaps, not findings that the companies have no products.

## Use in BizTrust

Keep this CSV separate from the application's synthetic catalog. For each candidate product, obtain insurer-approved policy wording and a current rate/quote method, then map benefit definitions and exclusions into the comparison model. Confirm the underwriting entity and distribution agreement before changing `review_status` or making a product visible to customers.
