## What the existing tests establish

The latest recorded flex-coverage verification reports **25 tests, 6 browser journeys and 19 automated accessibility checks passed**, including tenant/owner isolation, pricing integrity, simulator failures and configured quote-to-invoice persistence. The browser report contains no page/asset errors. See [flex pricing evidence](flex-coverage-pricing.md) and ignored `output/playwright/results.json`.

Those checks validate the implemented demonstration scope. They do not establish full-domain completion, a real payment/insurer integration, live Logto acceptance, a complete accessibility audit or production readiness. `docs/verification.md` retains an earlier 20-test milestone; the flex report is the later verification snapshot.

## Main dependencies and implementation sequence

1. **Broking operating model and catalogue governance:** confirm authority/product owners; model all actors, tenant relationships, cover types, approved products and rule/publication approvals. Build the staff console and permitted work queues/actions against those contracts.
2. **Insurer and finance lifecycle:** implement distinct submission/response/policy/evidence records, a reliable delivery worker, at least one real insurer sandbox and payment sandbox, reconciliation/ledger/refund workflows and customer notifications. Expand provider-by-provider and product-by-product.
3. **Production acceptance:** configure and verify customer/staff Logto and MFA, complete reviewed bilingual/legal content, deploy APISIX/TLS and private infrastructure, central monitoring/secrets/backups, restore drills and staged CI/CD with rollback.

Owners and required evidence are already listed in [the decision register](discovery.md). Development of the missing internal workflows can continue while provider and business decisions are resolved.

Production activation is explicitly blocked by [server configuration](../server/config.ts): `DEMO_MODE=false` throws until approved catalogue, provider integrations, identity acceptance and operational evidence exist.
